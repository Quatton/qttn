## Cushion is the best update to Minecraft in 2026

Hear me out. I know this seems completely out of place, but hang tight.
---

Below is the video[^3] if you want to gain some context, but essentially is a entity-based colored seat you can place at any sub-pixel height without ground support.

Minecraft has always been a sandbox game.

A static seating moving faster than a electric-powered minecart or a flying elytra doesn't kill the motive of playing the game.

The same goes for players not just completing the playthrough after they "solved" the game with unlimited wood, iron, and diamond armor, nor getting all the advancements.
